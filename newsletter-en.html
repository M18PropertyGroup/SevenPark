THE STORIES SHAPING SOUTH FLORIDA
Fed Hikes for the First Time Since 2023
The Fed raised rates to 3.75%–4% on Sept. 16, and the 30-year mortgage hit 7.40% before easing to 7.32% on Oct. 22. Financed buyers lose purchasing power, which favors cash buyers.
READ MORE
Florida Votes Nov. 3 on Amendment 3
The property-tax measure would raise the homestead exemption and cap non-homestead assessment growth at 5%. It needs 60%, and October polls range from 42% to 63%.
READ MORE
Miami-Dade Splits in Two
Condos sit at 12.3 months of supply with a $417,090 median, while 24 sales of $30M+ through September already beat last year’s record of 21.
