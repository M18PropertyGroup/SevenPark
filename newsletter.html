PRE-CONSTRUCTION OPPORTUNITY

SEVEN PARK

A New Residential Development in the Heart of Hallandale Beach

Hi there,

I wanted to share a pre-construction opportunity I’m following closely: Seven Park, a new 8-story residential development currently under construction in Hallandale Beach, just minutes from Aventura.

Designed with flexibility in mind, owners can use their residence as a primary home, second home, or take advantage of short- and long-term rental options, including Airbnb-style use. The project also features modern residences with high-end finishes, approximately 30,000 SF of amenities, and pricing positioned below many nearby Aventura properties.

With a new approximately $30 million public park directly across the street and continued investment throughout Hallandale, Seven Park is arriving in one of the area’s most actively developing neighborhoods.

Pricing

 

Current pricing discussed at the presentation ranges from approximately $500K to $650K+, depending on layout, floor, view, and availability.

Additional details discussed:

  HOA approximately $1.10/SF
  High-speed Wi-Fi included
  Electricity billed separately through FPL
  Assigned parking available

Pricing and inventory can change, so current availability should always be confirmed.
